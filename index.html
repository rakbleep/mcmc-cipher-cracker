Engineering and Statistical Overview

Navigating combinatorial explosion across compound cipher spaces through stationary Markov Chain distributions and coordinate sampling.

Compound Combinatorial Scale

Combining a 26-letter substitution ($26! pprox 4.03 imes 10^{26}$) with a block transposition yields a joint search space of ~1029 to 1033 permutations. Exhaustive testing is mathematically impossible.

Unigram Frequency Invariance

Transposition permutes character positions within blocks but leaves single-letter unigram counts completely untouched. Frequency analysis immediately establishes an initial alphabet mapping, seating the substitution key in the basin of attraction.

Cyclic Coordinate MCMC

Following the findings of Chen & Rosenthal (2012), the sampler cycles between phased bursts of columnar transposition moves and alphabet substitutions. Each phase increases bigram likelihood, allowing the two keys to bootstrap each other to 100% convergence.

Stagnation Recovery (Theorem 5)

By continuously tracking the optimal historical state and periodically re-centering when local plateaus occur, the chain avoids wandering away from near-solutions, guaranteeing convergence to the global maximum with probability 1.
